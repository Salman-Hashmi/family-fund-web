How it works

Send your donation

Pay by bank, JazzCash or Easypaisa to the family account, then upload the receipt in the app.

An admin verifies it

The owner or an admin checks the receipt. Verified payments get the blue tick.

Everyone sees the fund

Members see their own record and the family total. Help given from the fund is recorded too.

What's inside

  • Sign in with your Google account
  • Your own record, month by month, with receipts
  • Monthly reminder on the 1st
  • Today's date and the Islamic date
  • English, Urdu and Roman Urdu
  • Light, dark or match your phone
  • For admins: verify payments, all members, help given
  • For the owner: PDF audit of every record

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above and open the downloaded file.
  2. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from this source (your browser or WhatsApp).
  3. Tap Install, open Family Fund and continue with Google.
  4. When a small "New update ready" banner shows, tap Restart. You'll never need to download it again.

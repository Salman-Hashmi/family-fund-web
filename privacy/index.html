Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Family Fund is an app for one family to keep a shared record of its charity fund: who gave, how much, and how the money was used. It is made by Taaj Studio (Salman Taj, Pakistan). This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how you can have it removed.

What we store

WhatWhy
Your Google account name, email address and profile photoTo sign you in and link you to your family record. We never see or store your Google password.
Your name, username, phone number (optional) and profile photo (optional)So family members and admins can recognise you.
Your payments: amount, month, method (bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa, cash), transaction ID, notes, date and statusTo keep the family's donation record.
Receipt photos you uploadSo an admin can check the payment before verifying it.
Help given from the fund (who received it, amount, date, reason)Entered by admins to show how the fund is used.
In-app notificationsTo tell you when a payment is verified or a reminder is sent.
Settings on your phone (language, theme, reminders, vibration, Islamic date adjustment)Stored only on your phone, not on our servers.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, call logs, messages or bank login details. It does not move money: payments happen in your own bank or wallet app.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile, payments and receipts.
  • The owner and admins of your family fund can see every member's profile, payments and receipts, so they can verify payments and keep the record correct.
  • All members can see the family totals: the balance in the fund, the total collected, the total given in help, and the number of members.
  • Nobody outside your family fund can see your data. We do not sell or share it, and the app shows no ads.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), in Google's data centre region asia-south1 (Mumbai). It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules on the database decide who can read or change each record. App updates are delivered through Expo (expo.dev); they contain the app's code, not your data.

How long we keep it

Your data stays for as long as your family uses the fund, so the record stays complete. An admin can remove a member or a payment at any time.

Deleting your data

To have your account and records deleted, contact us on WhatsApp or email (below), or ask your family admin. We'll remove your profile, payments and receipts within 30 days. Family totals will no longer include your payments once they are deleted.

Children

The app is meant for adult family members. It is not designed for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let members know in the app.

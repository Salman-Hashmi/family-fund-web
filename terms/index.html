1. What the app does

Family Fund is a record book for a family's charity fund. It shows where to send donations, keeps a record of payments and receipts, and shows how the fund is used. The app does not hold, send or receive money. Every payment is made by you, in your own bank, JazzCash, Easypaisa or in cash, to the account the fund's owner publishes.

2. The fund and its admins

  • The fund belongs to the family and is managed by its owner and the admins the owner chooses.
  • Admins decide whether a payment is verified, and how money from the fund is given as help. Taaj Studio is not part of these decisions.
  • Donations are voluntary and there is no fixed amount unless your family agrees on one.

3. Your account
